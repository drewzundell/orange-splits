Team and help
  1. Open the PTTiming link above in your phone browser, men's race, the split you want.
  2. Long-press a result, drag to select the whole list (or Select All), Copy.
  3. Paste here. It reads automatically and sends the split to everyone who has the site open.
  4. Paste the full field for that split, not just the top 50, so every team's five count.
